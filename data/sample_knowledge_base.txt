1. Annual Leave & Time Off Policy:
All full-time employees are credited with 25 working days of paid annual leave per calendar year.
A maximum of 5 unused days may be rolled over into the subsequent financial year with managerial approval.

2. Remote Work & Hybrid Schedule:

3. Health & Wellness Benefits:
Comprehensive medical, dental, and vision insurance is provided from Day 1 of employment. 
Preventative dental checkups and annual OPG X-ray screenings are 100% covered under the health network.
